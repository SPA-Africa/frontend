# Kinyarwanda glossary

Every Kinyarwanda string in `lib/i18n/messages/*.ts` (the `rw` field) uses these terms, so the same idea always has the same word. If a reviewer changes a term here, search and replace it across the messages files too.

**Review status:** drafted with care but not yet reviewed by a native speaker. Please have a fluent Kinyarwanda speaker on the team review the strings before launch, starting with sign-up, the course player and anything legal (consent, data deletion, invoices).

## Style

- Address the user with the singular *wowe* forms (*Injira*, *Bika*, *Ohereza*), as Irembo and most Rwandan apps do. Use plural only when speaking to a group (*Murakaza neza* on public pages).
- Keep it plain and short. Buttons are verbs in the imperative (*Bika*, *Emeza*, *Siba*).
- Keep proper names, programme names and course titles in English unless they have an official Kinyarwanda name.
- Keep these as they are: SHAPE, SPA, CBC, PDF, CSV, KPI, AI, RWF, USD, email addresses, codes such as `PITCH-2026-00417`.
- Numbers and dates follow the data; do not translate placeholders such as `{name}`, `{n}`, `{date}`.
- Months, if written out: Mutarama, Gashyantare, Werurwe, Mata, Gicurasi, Kamena, Nyakanga, Kanama, Nzeri, Ukwakira, Ugushyingo, Ukuboza.
- No em or en dashes.
